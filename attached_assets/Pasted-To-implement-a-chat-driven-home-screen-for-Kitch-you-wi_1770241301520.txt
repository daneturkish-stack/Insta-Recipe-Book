Why Chat-First is the Winning Move
 * Higher Engagement: By 2026, industry data shows that AI chatbot traffic is more engaged, with longer session durations than traditional search.
 * Intent-Based Discovery: AI search understands the "concept" of user intent, allowing users to find specific movies or recipes using random elements or fragments of memory.
 * Better Conversion: AI-driven traffic achieves significantly higher conversion rates—up to 4.4x better than traditional organic search—because it delivers exactly what the user is looking for.
UX Best Practices for 2026
 * Guided Conversation: Provide clear conversation options to prevent "blank page syndrome" and keep interactions concise.
 * Explainability: Ensure the interface explains why it made a specific suggestion (e.g., "I suggested this because you have eggs in your Pantry") to build user trust.
 * Error Recovery: No AI is perfect; always include a fallback option or an easy way for users to override the AI's filters.
